Over 30 years of experience

Our goals

Our well established company was born in 1991 out of a desire of protecting and preventing the neighbours of Torrevieja. Our utmost goal is to make our city a safe place for residents and visitors. Therefore, we put quality and warranty as our most cared values.

At our company, we believe that the things that protect your home may also make it look better. We design and supply high-quality metal products for homes and businesses, including fences, window grilles, and doors, all built to last. Every piece is crafted from tough steel, aluminum, or wrought iron and finished with rust-resistant coatings, so it holds up through years of weather with very little upkeep.

Our range covers everything from clean, modern lines to classic ornamental designs, and we can custom-make products to fit your exact measurements and style. Whether you need stronger security, a smarter way to define your property, or a front door that makes a statement, our team is here to guide you from the first idea through to installation.

Three Decades of Craftsmanship

Mazon opened its doors thirty years ago as a small workshop with a simple idea: build metalwork that people can trust for a lifetime. Since then, we have grown steadily, but our approach has never changed. Every fence, window grille, and door that leaves our workshop carries the same care and attention to detail we started with.

Over the years, we have protected and improved thousands of homes and businesses. That experience shows in the details: the right weld, the right finish, the right fit. We have seen how metal performs through decades of sun, rain, and frost, and we use that knowledge to build stronger products.

Many of our customers have been with us since the beginning, and many of the first pieces we installed are still standing today. Some now come back for their children’s homes, or recommend us to neighbors and friends. For us, there is no better proof of quality than that.

Tradition guides how we work, but we never stand still. We combine time-honored techniques with modern tools and finishes, so you get the best of both worlds.
